## 2. Geometry

- Overall shape: a single turn-down collar unit consisting of a collar stand (band) and a wider collar leaf that curves outward from the neckline and narrows into two front points.
- Symmetry: bilaterally symmetric about the center-front vertical axis; the left and right leaf panels mirror each other in outline.
- Collar stand: visible as a narrower band along the edge adjacent to the neckline, distinct from the wider leaf section beyond it.
- Collar spread: the two front leaf points meet and slightly overlap at center front, leaving a small triangular gap directly below the meeting point.
- Tip shape: each front point ends in a continuous rounded curve — the outer edge curves down and turns toward the center-front edge without forming a sharp angled corner.
- Tip radius: the curve at each tip is a smooth, continuous arc (no vertex formed by two straight edges meeting is visible).
- Opening angle: the neckline edge forms one continuous open arc, spanning from one front point, around the back, to the other front point.
- Curvature: the outer (leaf) edge curves continuously from the back of the neck down toward each front point, tightening as it approaches the tip.
- Proportions: the leaf extends noticeably farther from the neckline edge than the stand's own band width — the leaf is proportionally wider/longer than the stand is tall.

## 3. Construction

- Stiffness: the collar holds its curved shape unsupported, indicating an internal reinforcing layer.
- Thickness: layered construction — a distinct outer face fabric and an interior-facing fabric of different texture/tone are visible at the neckline edge and along the inside of the curve.
- Folded edge: the outer perimeter edge of the leaf is a cleanly closed folded edge (no raw/unfinished edge visible along the outer curve or at either tip).
- Seam visibility: a topstitch line runs parallel to the outer folded edge, continuous along the leaf perimeter including around both tips.
- Transition: a horizontal seam/fold line separates the stand section from the leaf section.
- Center front join: the two leaf panels meet and slightly overlap at center front, with a visible vertical seam/fold at the point of overlap.
- Interfacing: the collar's ability to hold a curved, unsupported shape indicates an internal reinforcing layer (interfacing) is present within both the stand and the leaf.

## 4. Anchor Point

- Neckline: the long open arc edge (opposite the two front points) is the attachment edge that joins to the garment's neckline seam.
- Center placket: the two front points meet at the point corresponding to the garment's center-front placket opening.
- Shoulder transition: the neckline attachment edge runs continuously from one front point, through the positions corresponding to the shoulder seams, to center back, and on to the other front point.
- Symmetry axis: the vertical line passing through the center-front meeting point and the center-back midpoint of the neckline arc.

## 5. Constraint

- The two front tips must remain rounded — they must not be altered into a pointed or sharply angled corner.
- The left and right leaf panels must remain mirror-symmetric about the center-front axis.
- The stand-to-leaf proportion (leaf wider/longer than stand) must not change.
- The degree of overlap between the two front points at center front must be preserved as shown in the reference.
- The continuous single-arc shape of the neckline edge must not be broken or segmented.
